# Pricing

Nimbus offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Business. The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage at no cost. The Pro plan costs $0.02 per GB per month with no minimum. The Business plan costs $0.015 per GB per month with a 1 TB minimum. Egress traffic (downloads) is free for the first 100 GB each month and costs $0.05 per GB after that.
